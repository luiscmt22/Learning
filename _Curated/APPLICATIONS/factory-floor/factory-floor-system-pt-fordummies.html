Sistema de Produção de Chão de Fábrica — Guia Para Totós (PT)

Portuguese 'For Dummies' style walkthrough of the BioBraga Factory Floor production tracking system — explaining Factory_New.razor, MaterialConsumptionModal.razor, and FactoryFloorService.cs as a restaurant kitchen metaphor with a day-in-the-life worker scenario.

What if the difference between 'making 50 bottles' and 'the company accountant knowing you made 50 bottles, updating the warehouse, and invoicing the customer' was one click from João with a smudged PIN?

Executive summary

A non-technical tour of the Factory Floor production tracking system described through the story of João the machine operator: he opens the page, selects his machine, clicks Start on an order, pauses for lunch (triggering the Material Consumption modal), and clicks Stop at the end. The doc teaches what each of the three main files does (the dashboard page, the material consumption modal, the service brain) using analogies to a restaurant kitchen order screen, a chef's prep sheet, and an efficient office manager. Explains smart features like historical-average material suggestions, automatic stock deduction, Primavera accounting integration, and PIN-gated actions without showing any code.

💡
MetaphorThe Factory Floor system is a smart kitchen. Factory_New.razor is the order-ticket screen above the pass. The Material Consumption modal is the chef's prep sheet plus the 'what we actually used' tally. FactoryFloorService is the invisible sous chef who, the moment the ticket comes in, starts the timer, updates the mise en place count, and silently WhatsApps the accountant.

1Why it exists

Before this system, production was tracked on paper: workers would scribble quantities and material use on sheets that got lost, managers had no real-time visibility into production, stock counts were always wrong because nobody logged material consumption accurately, and accounting only learned about production at month-end. The system exists to digitize all of that: every production order start, pause, stop, and material confirmation is captured at the exact moment it happens, stock counts update automatically, and accounting gets the data instantly via Primavera integration.

2What it does

Provides three coordinated pieces. Factory_New.razor is the shop-floor dashboard — shows today's stats (produced/remaining), a machine selector, and two columns of work orders (Por Iniciar / Iniciadas). Workers click Start/Pause/Stop on order cards. MaterialConsumptionModal.razor appears whenever a worker pauses or finishes an order — it collects units produced, waste count, and a table of materials consumed (pre-filled with calculated suggestions based on produced quantity and historical averages, with red warnings when stock is low). FactoryFloorService.cs is the invisible logic layer that marks orders in-progress, deducts materials from stock, adds finished goods to inventory, records waste, and pushes everything to Primavera accounting.

3How it works

The day-in-the-life scenario: João arrives at 8:00, opens the system on a factory tablet, enters his PIN, selects Machine #3, and sees Order #1234 (make 100 plastic bottles). He clicks Start, enters his PIN, and the order moves from Por Iniciar to Iniciadas with a timestamp. At 12:00 he pauses for lunch — the material modal appears, he enters '60 bottles produced', the system suggests '6kg of plastic used' based on proportional calculation, he confirms, and the system atomically deducts 6kg from warehouse stock, adds 60 bottles to finished goods, updates the order to 40 remaining, and records the timestamp. After lunch he resumes, finishes the order at 14:30, clicks Stop, enters 40 produced and 2 waste, and the order marks Completa. Meanwhile Gestora Maria sees all of this live on her dashboard. Smart checks prevent impossible orders (not enough stock), prevent negative quantities, and require PIN on every significant action.

             o Introduz: "60 garrafas produzidas"

    Porque É Que Isto É Brilhante



Para os Trabalhadores:



    • Sem papelada - tudo é digital

    • Não podem esquecer passos - o sistema guia-os

    • Feedback instantâneo - sabem se estão a fazer bem

    • Menos culpa - o sistema controla tudo de forma justa



Para os Gestores:



    • Visibilidade em tempo real - veem a produção ao vivo

    • Custos precisos - sabem exatamente o que foi usado

    • Detetar problemas cedo - uso anormal de material = sinal vermelho

    • Melhor planeamento - dados históricos ajudam a prever necessidades futuras



Para a Empresa:



    • Menos desperdício - controlar e reduzir perda de material

    • Melhor inventário - níveis de stock sempre precisos

    • Faturação mais rápida - integração automática com contabilidade

    • Controlo de qualidade - saber que lotes tiveram problemas



    Cenários Comuns e Soluções



"Ups, introduzi quantidade errada!"



    • Antes de confirmar: Basta mudar o número

    • Depois de confirmar: O supervisor pode criar ajuste



"A máquina avariou durante a produção!"



    • Carregar "Pausar" → Registar o que foi feito até agora

    • A manutenção arranja a máquina

    • Carregar "Iniciar" novamente para continuar



"Ficámos sem material!"



    • O sistema mostra aviso vermelho antes de começar

    • Não pode prosseguir sem autorização do supervisor

    • Previne início de ordens impossíveis



"Esqueci-me do meu PIN!"

Key takeaways

  • Digitizing shop-floor tracking moves production data from 'end-of-month guess' to 'live, accurate, per-action'
  • PIN gating on every Start/Pause/Stop action is the shop-floor equivalent of a time clock plus a signature
  • Smart material suggestions based on historical averages reduce data-entry friction while still letting workers override
  • One action in the UI (Confirm Production) triggers four downstream updates: stock deduction, finished-goods addition, waste log, and Primavera accounting push
  • Preventing impossible orders (not enough stock) at the UI layer is cheaper than rolling back a bad production run
